The Three-Stage Redressal Process

[ Operational Framework ]
[ 01 ]

Formal Registration

The student selects the exact department unit, defines priority, and documents the incident with date, location, and factual context.

[ 02 ]

Departmental Inquiry

The grievance is routed directly to the designated faculty supervisor or facility warden for on-site inspection and investigation.

[ 03 ]

Verified Resolution

Corrective action is documented in an audit log, comments are opened for review, and an official printable receipt is generated.

🚨 24x7 Anti-Ragging & Emergency Proctorial Line

The university enforces an uncompromising zero-tolerance policy regarding harassment, ragging, or physical safety hazards. Emergency dispatches trigger instant proctorial escalation.
